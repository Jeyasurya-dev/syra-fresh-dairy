Home/About

SYRA Fresh began in 2019 with one goal: cut out the middlemen between local farms and your kitchen. What started as a single milk delivery route in one neighborhood is now a daily cold-chain network delivering dairy, ice cream, healthy snacks and fresh fruit across the city — usually before you've had your morning coffee.

How it works

Every evening, our sourcing team places orders with partner farms and dairies for next-day delivery. Produce and dairy arrive at our micro-fulfilment hubs before sunrise, get quality-checked, and go straight into insulated delivery bags. There's no long-term storage — what you receive was harvested or processed within the last 24–48 hours.

Our promise

  • Transparent sourcing — every product page tells you where it comes from.
  • Hassle-free returns if anything arrives below our quality bar.
  • Fair, consistent pricing with no surge charges during festivals.
